My Dearest,

Happy birthday baby nakun, palangga nakun, lablab nakuuun. Guwang kanaaa HAHAHAHAHA. Pwede kana ma minyoan nakuun hehe.

Sorry lablab, as of now I cannot give you anything you want, especially sa imo birthday. I am aware nga dili ko perfect nga pagka partner, I commit mistakes and sometimes dili ko showy sa imo, but one thing is for sure, love kaayu tikaw. I may not always know the right words to say, pero I hope makita nimo sa akun actions kung nanu ka kaimportante sa akun. I want to be someone nga pwede nimo duolan whenever you're tired, sad, or having a bad day. Someone nga mamati nimo, mosabot nimo, ug mo-support sa imo dreams.

On your birthday, I pray nga mahatag sa imo ni Lord ang happiness, good health, success, ug tanan nga imong gipangandoy. You deserve all the good things in life, baby, kay kaybaw ako kung nanu ka ka-strong bisan sa mga times nga kapoy kana. Always remember nga proud kaayo ako sa imo, and I'll always be here cheering for you.

Thank you for coming into my life, baby. Thank you sa imo love, patience, ug sa tanan nimo efforts para sa atun. Dili man natun malikayan nga ara kitay misunderstandings usahay, pero I hope we continue to choose each other and work things out together. I want more birthdays with you, more memories, more laughs, and more moments nga kita ra duha.

I love you so much, my pretty girl. Enjoy your day, baby. I hope I can make you feel as loved as you make me feel. Always remember nga ara lako permi . Ikaw akong baby, akong love, ug akong favorite person.

Happy birthday, my love. I love you always. ♡

Forever yours, Your Man ♡
